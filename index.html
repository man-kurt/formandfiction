01 / The approach

Not software for its own sake

3D modelling is a way of constructing images, bodies, environments, narratives and possible worlds.

My teaching combines practical knowledge of 3D production with contemporary art, critical theory, decolonial thinking, moving image and installation.

The focus is on developing a visual language of your own.

04 / Teaching philosophy

Concept before technique

My practice developed through contemporary art, site-specific work, critical curatorial studies, environmental engineering and digital production.

Teaching is a way of sharing methods, opening processes and helping others develop their own relationship with digital tools.

You do not need to reproduce my aesthetic. The aim is to find a visual and conceptual language of your own.

05 / About Sara

Artist, theorist, 3D practitioner

Sara Bissen is an interdisciplinary artist and theorist working with 3D animation, immersive installation, electronic sculpture and research-based storytelling.

Her work explores mythology, identity, cultural memory, colonial histories, gender, technology and the politics of representation.

Her work has been presented at Centre Pompidou-Metz, PACE Gallery New York, Halle Nord Geneva, Ujazdów Castle Warsaw and other international venues.
